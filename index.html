    Aadhaar compliance support

    Get your Aadhaar integration audit-ready

    Entities that authenticate residents through Aadhaar carry obligations under the Aadhaar Act, 2016 and UIDAI's authentication regulations. We help you meet them in your architecture, your processes and your paperwork.

    AUA / KUA

    Authentication and e-KYC user agenciesDirect agreement with UIDAI. We review your end-to-end flow, key management and the controls UIDAI checks at annual audit.

    Sub-AUA / Sub-KUA

    Departments and businesses authenticating through an AUACommon for state schemes and welfare portals. We prepare the compliance checklist, evidence and application-level controls your AUA asks for.

    ASA

    Authentication service agenciesNetwork and infrastructure providers connecting AUAs to the CIDR. We review connectivity, encryption and operational security.

    Applications

    Portals and apps that collect or display AadhaarWe test the application itself for data exposure, insecure storage and broken masking.

    Controls we help you put in place

    • Aadhaar Data VaultAadhaar numbers stored only in the vault, referenced elsewhere by a reference key
    • HSM-backed key managementEncryption keys generated and held in a hardware security module
    • Informed consentConsent captured in the resident's language and recorded before every authentication
    • Masking and minimal displayOnly the last four digits shown on screens, reports and printouts
    • Registered biometric devicesDevice certification, PID block encryption and no storage of core biometrics
    • Authentication logsTransaction logs kept for the periods the regulations specify, without PID data
    • Grievance redressal and VID supportResident-facing processes and Virtual ID acceptance

    The formal annual audit for UIDAI-regulated entities must be performed by an auditor who meets UIDAI's eligibility criteria. Vyash delivers readiness, remediation and documentation, and can coordinate the formal audit with an eligible auditor on your behalf.

    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Is Vyash a CERT-In empanelled auditor?

    No. Audits that must be done by a CERT-In empanelled organisation, such as many government website and application audits, need an empanelled auditor to sign the final report. We do the preparation: testing, remediation guidance and retesting, so the formal audit goes through cleanly. For private-sector clients, our VAPT and IS audit reports stand on their own.

    Can you issue an ISO 27001 certificate?

    No consultant can. Certificates are issued by accredited certification bodies. We build and run your ISMS with you, conduct the internal audit and support you through the Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits.

    We use Aadhaar authentication through another agency. Do we still have obligations?

    Yes. Sub-AUAs and Sub-KUAs must follow the same core rules on consent, storage, masking and logging, and your AUA will usually ask you for a completed compliance checklist and supporting evidence. We help you prepare both.

    How long does ISO 27001 implementation take?

    It depends on the scope, how much is already documented and how quickly your team can act on findings. We give you a timeline after the gap assessment, based on what we actually find.

    Does the DPDP Act apply to us?

    If you process digital personal data of individuals in India, it very likely does. Obligations under the DPDP Rules come into force in phases, so a readiness assessment now gives you time to plan the changes to consent, security and breach reporting.

    What do we receive at the end of a VAPT?

    A report with an executive summary, each finding with its severity, affected asset, steps to reproduce, evidence and a recommended fix, followed by a retest report once you've remediated.
